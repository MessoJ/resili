# Do No Harm

resili supports, rather than replaces, Kenyan public warning authorities.

- **False alerts:** publish probabilistic language, data source, expiry, and
  uncertainty; require review before high-impact delivery.
- **Spoofing:** sign alerts, verify delivery integrations, and expose an audit
  trail without PII.
- **Exclusion:** make trigger rules inspectable, record delivery failures, and
  support low-bandwidth and ward-committee dissemination paths.
- **Discrimination:** evaluate coverage and false-alarm disparities before
  deployment; do not use protected traits for eligibility.
- **Public ownership:** use open standards and a permissive licence, document
  data extraction, and provide a county handover path rather than lock-in.
